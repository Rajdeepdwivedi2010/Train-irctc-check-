What the status codes mean

The codes on your ticket, in plain words. A smaller waitlist number means better odds.

CNF
Confirmed. Coach and berth are allotted.
RAC
Reservation Against Cancellation. You can board and share a berth, and may move to a full berth if others cancel.
GNWL
General Waiting List. The most common waitlist, usually with the best odds of clearing.
RLWL
Remote Location Waiting List. For journeys starting or ending at an intermediate station on the route.
PQWL
Pooled Quota Waiting List. A shared pool for shorter trips between intermediate stations; odds are usually lower.
TQWL
Tatkal Waiting List. A waitlist under the Tatkal quota, formerly shown as CKWL.
